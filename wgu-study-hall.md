# WGU Study Hall for ChatGPT

Prepare for upcoming WGU courses with a reusable AI-powered Study Hall.

WGU Study Hall was created and tested primarily with **ChatGPT** and is designed to work with courses across WGU degree programs.

## Get Started

### 1. Download WGU Study Hall

[**→ Download `study-hall.md`**](https://github.com/ab45272/wgu-lab/releases/latest/download/study-hall.md)

### 2. Open ChatGPT

Start a new ChatGPT conversation and upload the downloaded `study-hall.md` file.

### 3. Start Study Hall

Say:

**Start Study Hall.**

That's it.

Study Hall will guide you through the rest of the setup and begin preparing you for your upcoming courses.

## About WGU Study Hall

WGU Study Hall is an unofficial, student-created learning tool.

It is not created, endorsed, approved, or maintained by Western Governors University.

Students are responsible for following current WGU academic-integrity requirements, AI-use policies, assessment rules, and course-specific instructions.
